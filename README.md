A make.fun bounty entry: two lines to my past self.

- **Line 1** warns my past self about one specific moment: the "quick fix" at 2 a.m.
- **Line 2** says why that night mattered: it taught me sleep is a debugging tool.

## Entry

> Don't trust the 'quick fix' at 2 a.m. — the bug you patch half-asleep will wake you up three more times before sunrise.
> That night mattered because it taught me sleep is a debugging tool, and ever since, I fix nothing after midnight.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the two lines to `/output/entry.txt`.
